Plan the move

The SETUP framework

Use the framework to prepare a JS-SEZ expansion from zone selection through workforce readiness.

  1. S

    Select your zone strategically

    Compare targeted economic activity and choose the zone that suits your needs.

  2. E

    Evaluate incentives

    Review tax incentives and facilitation support from MIDA, IRDA and EnterpriseSG.

  3. T

    Take the right legal steps

    Choose a business structure, register the company, obtain licences and meet tax and customs requirements.

  4. U

    Utilise bilateral support

    Use regional banking, SMF and the Federation of Malaysian Manufacturers for market entry and partnership support.

  5. P

    Prepare your people and supply chain

    Plan skilled worker mobility, engineering and technical talent, workforce training and local supply chain partnerships.

Source: SMF JS-SEZ Playbook, pages 6-7.

Before you commit

Incentives and tax watch-outs

Plan the operating model and understand how incentive schemes fit before relying on them.

Malaysia incentives

Targeted JS-SEZ incentives apply mainly to sectors including manufacturing, smart logistics and global services. Smaller investments can still access standard Malaysian investment incentives.

Source: page 8.

Tax watch-outs

The playbook highlights 5% corporate tax for 10 or 15 years for qualifying companies, and 5% personal income tax for knowledge workers. It also warns that business structure can affect tax exposure, transfer pricing and regulatory compliance.

Source: page 3 and page 6.

Bring your team

Passes for staff

The appendix describes different pass types for expatriate posts, temporary professional work and business travel.

Employment Pass

MIDA assesses expatriate posts as Key Posts or Term Posts. The playbook lists basic salary categories of RM10,000; RM5,000-RM9,999; and RM3,000-RM4,999.

Professional Visit Pass

For temporary, specific professional purposes such as training, consultancy or project-based work. It can be for up to 12 months and is not extendable.

Investor Pass

For business travellers from the qualifying investor categories, subject to the stated stay and application requirements.

Source: SMF JS-SEZ Playbook, pages 28-29.
